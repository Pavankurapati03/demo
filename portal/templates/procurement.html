Business Context

Accuracy: Accuracy measures overall operational correctness. The percentage of all business decisions, forecasts, or classifications that are completely correct.

Precision: Precision measures the quality and reliability of positive decisions. The fraction of positive predictions that turned out to be actual real positives (minimizing false alarms and knowing how much false alarms cost).

Recall: Recall measures the completeness of capturing all target opportunities or risks. Out of all the actual opportunities or risks that existed, how many did we successfully catch (and how much does a missed opportunity cost us)?

F1 Score: F1 score is a single number that balances precision and recall. It forces a compromise between Precision and Recall balancing overall performance.

AUC: AUC measures a model's ability to rank items from most likely to least likely. It tells you if your system can successfully separate the good from the bad across the board.
